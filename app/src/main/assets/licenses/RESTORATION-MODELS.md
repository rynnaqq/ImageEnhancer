# Local restoration model bank

All models are bundled with the installed app. SHA-256, part digests, versions and tensor contracts are recorded in models/manifest.json. There is no runtime download or remote inference.

- YuNet: OpenCV Zoo MIT model, revision 47534e27c9851bb1128ccc0102f1145e27f23f98. https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/47534e27c9851bb1128ccc0102f1145e27f23f98/models/face_detection_yunet . Face boxes and five landmarks only; no recognition or identity embeddings.
- SwinIR: Jingyun Liang and contributors' Apache-2.0 real-world x4 network, exported from official source revision 6545850fbf8df298df73d81f3e8cba638787c8bd and checkpoint 003_realSR_BSRGAN_DFO_s64w8_SwinIR-M_x4_GAN.pth. https://github.com/JingyunLiang/SwinIR . Fixed96 RGB input,384 output, used on detected face regions. It enhances neural face detail; it is a general real-image SR model and does not guarantee identity recovery. Full conversion/parity provenance is archived in SWINIR-PROVENANCE.json.
- DDColor: Xiaoyang Kang and contributors' Apache-2.0 learned chroma model. Original authors publish Apache-2.0 weights at https://huggingface.co/piddnad/DDColor-models . The pinned Edgetools FP16 ONNX conversion retains float32 I/O: https://huggingface.co/edgetools/ddcolor/tree/c4c98361d19eda29908cc960be39e8eeb44fc530 . Its full model card and architecture/checkpoint lineage are archived in DDCOLOR-MODEL-CARD.md. Original lightness and alpha are retained when assembling color predictions; gamut clipping can cause small lightness differences. Predicted colors are plausible, not historical evidence.
- LaMa: Samsung Research / Roman Suvorov and collaborators' Apache-2.0 masked inpainting network. Original source https://github.com/advimman/lama . Carve's published FP32 opset17 conversion: https://huggingface.co/Carve/LaMa-ONNX/tree/c3c0c9e468934d62e79c329e35d82dd09ff8c444 . This app chooses the FP32 model after comparison with OpenCV's quantized derivative. It predicts selected damaged areas; pixels outside the selected mask remain exact in the repair stage. Reconstructed regions are recorded in private result companions and protected from repeated face processing.

Apache-2.0 and MIT license texts are archived beside this file. The original ESPCN and ONNX Runtime attributions remain included. The classical scratch detector supplies a conservative damage mask to the learned LaMa model; review damage selections and use the brush where automatic detection misses damage. The first face pass handles up to16 detected regions. Processing sessions are serial and memory checked; insufficient-memory errors can be retried after freeing RAM. All quality measurements are described in the repository validation documents.
